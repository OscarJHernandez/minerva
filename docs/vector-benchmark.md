## Limits

- Results are numerically checked against an independent Cartesian finite-difference evaluation; no symbolic proof is claimed for an evaluated result.
- A result that needs mixed partial derivatives to commute is withheld unless the field's smoothness is declared.
- The axis and origin of curvilinear systems are reported as restrictions; distributions (a delta at the origin) are not represented.
- At most four nested operators; three coordinate systems; no tensor fields, no integrals (later phases).
